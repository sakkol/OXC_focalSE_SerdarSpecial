# Efficacy and Safety of Oxcarbazepine Loading Dose in Patients with Focal Status Epilepticus (the Serdar special)



This project was completed at the University of Alabama at Birmingham to evaluate the efficacy and safety of oxcarbazepine (OXC) loading in patients who were admitted with focal status epilepticus. The OXC loading dose was colloquially referred to as the “Serdar special” within our Neurology department.



The dataset can be obtained from the first or last author of the study (Serdar Akkol, MD PhD or Kristina Stein, PharmD). The analysis script is shared within this folder.
